Shift assistant learning…

UPSHIFT @ —rpm · gear -
▬ power (hp)▬ torque (Nm) ● now┊ shift pt power & torque curves — upshift at the dashed line
The upshift point is the rpm where the next gear makes more wheel torque (engine Nm × gear ratio) than staying — so torque drives the maths; power (hp = Nm × rpm) is the same data shown a second way. Learns your torque curve × per-gear ratios from full-throttle, going-straight data and re-optimises every frame. Only counts while rolling at full throttle with the clutch out — standing-still revs, clutch-in, and the moment of each shift are ignored. Do clean pulls all the way to the redline in each gear so the top of the curve is learned. Gears you haven't driven yet get a ~predicted shift point, forecast from the ratios of the gears you have — it firms up to live once you drive that gear.

Recent fumbles

A shift is clean when your throttle drops below the threshold during the gear change — i.e. you actually lifted off. If the gas stays in, it's a miss. Each fumble logs the gear change, the throttle still applied, and the rpm. Upshifts drive the headline score; downshifts are tracked separately because a rev-match blip legitimately shows throttle. Threshold changes apply to new shifts; stats persist in this browser — hit reset to start fresh.
